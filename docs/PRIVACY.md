# Privacy Policy — PIANO by Eu De

**Last updated:** 2026-10-24  
**App:** PIANO by Eu De (`com.be314.piano`)  
**Developer:** Eu De / bea314

## English

### Summary

PIANO by Eu De is a simple piano keyboard app. It does not require an account, does not show ads, and does not collect personal data for its core functionality.

### Data we process

- **Audio:** Notes you play are synthesized on your device. We do not record or upload your playing.
- **No account:** The app does not ask you to sign in.
- **No sensitive permissions:** The current version does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone (for recording), or similar permissions for app features.

### Data stored on your device

- Standard app data needed to run Flutter/Android (e.g. app cache). Nothing is synced to our servers by the app itself.

### Third parties

- **Google Play:** If you install from Google Play, Google’s terms and privacy policy apply to the store and distribution.
- We do not embed third-party analytics or advertising SDKs in the current release.

### Children

The app is suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

### Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. For Play Store listing, host this document at a public HTTPS URL and paste that URL in Play Console.

### Contact

For privacy questions: open an issue on the project repository or contact the developer via the email listed on the Play Store listing.
